FAQ

Questions, answered honestly.

What is the Frontline World Training Academy?

The learning arm of Frontline World. It turns the firm's daily research, foresight, communication, and digital practice into open-enrolment courses and bespoke programmes that build institutional capability.

Who are the programmes for?

Individuals — leaders, executives, managers, researchers, government and NGO staff, and communication professionals. And organisations — universities, governments, international and non-governmental organisations, associations, and corporate teams.

How do I apply?

Use the application form on the contact page, select Course Application, and name the programme you're applying for. We'll confirm next steps and details by email.

How much do programmes cost?

Pricing is being confirmed for the launch catalogue and will be published per course. For now, prices are shared on request — enrol your interest and we'll respond with full details.

What formats do programmes run in?

Online, in person, hybrid, and in-house. Each course page lists its formats under Format.

When do the first cohorts run?

Dates are being confirmed. Enrol your interest through the application form and we'll notify you when enrolment opens.

Do I need prior experience to join?

Each course page states who it is designed for. Courses are open by default; where prerequisites apply they will be listed on the course page.

Can you build a programme for our organisation?

Yes. Corporate and organisational programmes are designed around your context, your people, and your strategic challenges — diagnosed, designed, and delivered in-house or internationally.

Are programmes certified?

Certificate details are being confirmed and will be published per programme when finalised.
