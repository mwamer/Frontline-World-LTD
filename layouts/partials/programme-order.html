{{- /* The catalogue's programme order: every published programme, in the order the programme_families vocabulary lists its families. The families come from data/vocab/associates.yml, so the order follows the vocabulary rather than a page weight, and a programme in the sixth family appears after the fifth without anyone numbering it. Programmes are then taken in the order their own pages are returned within each family. The family is the sort key and nothing more: it is not rendered, and a programme's family never appears on the page. Grouping therefore orders the list without publishing the grouping, and a family holding several programmes keeps them adjacent with no change here. Both Academy sections order themselves from this, so the programme cards and the course cards beneath them read in the same sequence. Ordering a course by its programme is what keeps the flat course list from becoming a flat, unrelated list. */ -}} {{- $families := index hugo.Data.vocab.associates "programme_families" | default slice -}} {{- $programmes := where site.RegularPages "Section" "programmes" -}} {{- $order := slice -}} {{- range $family := $families -}} {{- range where $programmes "Params.family" $family.id -}} {{- $order = $order | append .Params.slug -}} {{- end -}} {{- end -}} {{- return $order -}}
