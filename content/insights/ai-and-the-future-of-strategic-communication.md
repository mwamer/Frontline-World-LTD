The rapid adoption of artificial intelligence is reshaping communication ecosystems across the world. AI-powered tools can create, amplify, and distribute content at unprecedented speed, influencing how individuals, organisations, and institutions engage with information. While these technologies offer significant opportunities for innovation, efficiency, and audience engagement, they also contribute to growing concerns surrounding misinformation, synthetic media, information manipulation, and declining public trust.
