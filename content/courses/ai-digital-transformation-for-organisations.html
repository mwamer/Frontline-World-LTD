Course overview

A practical course for leaders steering digital and AI adoption. It separates capability from noise, builds a working understanding of what emerging technologies can and cannot do, and gives leaders the frameworks to make sound investment and change decisions for their organisations.

Courses run in small cohorts, online and in person, and can be delivered in-house for organisations.

What the course covers

✓

Capability from noise

Separate what emerging technology can actually do from the hype around it.

✓

A working mental model of AI

Understand how the technology works at the level leaders need.

✓

Investment decisions

Frameworks for judging where digital and AI spend actually delivers value.

✓

Leading change

Steer adoption across the organisation without waiting for perfect clarity.

✓

Judgement, not jargon

Make confident calls on technology in language your board understands.
