Overview

Course overview

A course for leaders whose operating conditions rarely stay stable. It develops judgement, decision-making under ambiguity, and the communication and team leadership practices that hold organisations together through change and uncertainty.

Courses run in small cohorts, online and in person, and can be delivered in-house for organisations.

Content

What the course covers

  • 01

    Judgement

    Make decisions you can stand behind when the facts are incomplete.

  • 02

    Deciding under ambiguity

    Act decisively without waiting for certainty you won't get.

  • 03

    Holding people steady

    Team leadership practices that keep organisations together through change.

  • 04

    Communication in uncertainty

    Say what needs saying when no one has all the answers.

  • 05

    Humane, decisive leadership

    Combine clarity with care in unstable conditions.
