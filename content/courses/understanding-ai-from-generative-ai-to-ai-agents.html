Overview

Course overview

A foundation course for professionals who need to understand AI without becoming technologists. From large language models to autonomous agents, it explains how the technology works, where the value and the risks sit, and how to assess AI claims, opportunities, and implications with confidence.

Courses run in small cohorts, online and in person, and can be delivered in-house for organisations.
