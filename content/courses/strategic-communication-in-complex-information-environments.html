Overview

Course overview

A course for communicators operating in contested, fast-moving, and sensitive settings. It builds strategic communication skills grounded in the firm’s media advisory practice: narrative control, stakeholder engagement, crisis communication, and operating with credibility under scrutiny.

Courses run in small cohorts, online and in person, and can be delivered in-house for organisations.

What the course covers

    Narrative control

    Hold the story in contested, fast-moving settings.

  • 02

    Stakeholder engagement

    Map, listen to, and move the people who matter.

  • 03

    Crisis communication

    Communicate under pressure without adding risk.

  • 04

    Credibility under scrutiny

    Operate with authority when every word is examined.

  • 05

    Media practice

    Skills grounded in the firm’s media advisory work.
