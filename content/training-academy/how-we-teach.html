How We Teach

From the firm’s own work to professional practice.

Frontline World turns its own work into learning that can be applied to real decisions.

{{< learning-arc >}}

One line: from material to method, decision, and practice.

The model

Six movements, one continuous line

  1. 01

    Research

    Programmes begin in Frontline World’s research, foresight, communication, and digital practice.

    Where the material comes from

  2. 02

    Evidence

    Evidence is presented with its basis, limits, and implications.

    How the material is tested

  3. 03

    Analysis

    Methods become frameworks for working through decisions, not summaries of them.

    The shape of the method

  4. 04

    Practice

    Discussion and practical work come together around participants’ own decisions.

    Conditions of learning

  5. 05

    Application

    Methods are applied to participants’ institutional contexts, priorities, and constraints.

    Where the method is used

  6. 06

    Strategic reflection

    Reflection asks whether participants can explain their reasoning, hold a position, and act on it.

    What is taken away

Principles

Six principles, stated plainly

Six principles shape how Academy learning is designed.

  1. 01

    Research-informed

    Learning draws on Frontline World’s research, foresight, communication, and digital practice.

  2. 02

    Frameworks, not summaries

    Participants work with methods they can apply to decisions.

  3. 03

    Applied to real decisions

    Learning is connected to decisions participants actually face.

  4. 04

    Small cohorts

    Discussion and practical application sit together in small cohorts.

  5. 05

    Judgement over claims

    Participants examine what claims and methods support, including their limits.

  6. 06

    Capability that transfers

    The aim is capability that remains useful beyond the course.
