Service 01

Strategic Research & Institutional Advisory

Understand complexity. Inform better decisions.

We help governments, public institutions, international organisations, NGOs, and companies understand complex operating environments, assess institutional realities, and turn evidence into strategic action.

We Provide

  • Strategic research and analysis
  • Policy and evidence reviews
  • Context and stakeholder assessments
  • Institutional diagnostics
  • Strategic planning support
  • Organisational assessments

Client Value

Clearer evidence. Better decisions. Stronger strategic direction.

Service 02

Risk & Strategic Foresight

Anticipate emerging risks, opportunities, and future scenarios.

We help leaders and organisations identify emerging risks, assess future developments, and explore alternative scenarios before they become crises. Our foresight services enable clients to move beyond reacting to change and begin preparing for it.

We Provide

  • Risk Assessments
  • Strategic Foresight
  • Scenario Planning
  • Horizon Scanning
  • Emerging Issues Analysis
  • Resilience Reviews
  • Preparedness Assessments
  • Future Trends Monitoring

Client Value

Increased resilience, stronger preparedness, and improved future readiness.
