Our Expertise

Multidisciplinary Knowledge Applied to Complex Challenges

Specialist Knowledge Areas

Our multidisciplinary team brings together deep expertise across multiple domains. This breadth enables us to assemble the right combination of skills for each client challenge and deliver integrated solutions that bridge research, policy, and practice.

Communication & Media

Strategic communications, media development, and narrative analysis

We help organisations communicate effectively and strategically in complex environments. Our expertise spans:

  • Strategic communication planning and audits
  • Media and narrative strategy
  • Crisis and reputational risk management
  • Stakeholder and audience engagement
  • Digital and social media strategy
  • Institutional communications

Digital Transformation

Digital strategy, technology adoption, and digital-enabled change

We support organisations in navigating digital change and building digital capability. Our work includes:

  • Digital strategy and roadmaps
  • Technology assessment and adoption
  • AI and emerging technology integration
  • Digital capability building
  • Change management for digital initiatives
  • Digital governance and policy

Governance

Democratic governance, policy development, and institutional reform

We bring deep knowledge of governance systems and institutional development. Our expertise encompasses:

  • Governance reform and institutional design

  • Anti-corruption and integrity systems
  • Public sector management
  • Stakeholder engagement in governance

Conflict & Peace

Conflict analysis, peacebuilding, and post-conflict recovery

We work in complex conflict and post-conflict contexts, drawing on extensive field experience. Our capabilities include:

  • Conflict analysis and assessment

Organizational Resilience

  • Risk and vulnerability assessment

How We Combine Expertise

These eight areas of specialist knowledge are not siloed. Rather, they work together in integrated ways to address complex challenges:

Example: Crisis Response

Working in Complex Environments

A defining characteristic of FW's expertise is our deep experience working in fragile, conflict-affected, and rapidly changing environments. This shapes how we apply our knowledge:

  • Context-Sensitive: We understand local dynamics, political sensitivities, and operating constraints
  • Risk-Aware: We design solutions that account for security, political, and institutional risks
  • Locally-Grounded: We work with local partners and build local capacity rather than imposing external solutions
  • Adaptive: We design flexible approaches that can adjust to changing circumstances
  • Evidence-Based: We ground recommendations in rigorous research and local knowledge
